More info: How was the board established?

Phase 1: Feedback on board structure

In this phase, we asked feedback on a first proposal of the board structure (v1).
The options were:
 1. Approve without modifications.
 2. Approve with modifications.
 3. No approval. Need for a new review round.

Phase 2: Filling board positions

Phase 2a: Nominations

In this phase, we invited anyone to nominate themselves or others for any of the roles specified in the final iAIMS board structure (v2).

Phase 2b: Voting

Finally, during the voting phase, people could answer "Yes", "Yes with suggetions" or "No" to the question "Do you approve this board?".
